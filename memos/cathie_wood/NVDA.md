2023-01-29 because revenue has run ahead of research, not because research was cut: R&D grew every year.
Today's sell-off in chips is the kind of volatility we lean into on a five-year view.

**What I'd need to see.** R&D share holding above the threshold as revenue scales, and growth that
broadens beyond a few data-center customers.
