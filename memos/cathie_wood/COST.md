# Cathie Wood memo: Costco Wholesale (COST)

**Decision: Pass**

**The checks.**
- Revenue grew 15% or more a year, first year to last: fail
- R&D 5% or more of revenue, latest year: not known

**Growth.** Revenue went from 226,954 to 303,154, and the script prints growth of 7.51% a year,
first year to last. That is a well-run compounder, but it is half the pace we look for. We invest
in platforms whose growth bends the curve over five years; this curve is a straight line.

**Research.** R&D is not found in any of the five years: Costco reports no
ResearchAndDevelopmentExpense tag, so its R&D share of revenue is not known. I won't guess it. A
warehouse retailer is not where the innovation platforms live.

**What I'd need to see.** Revenue growth that clears our threshold, or a disclosed R&D line that
shows Costco building technology, not just warehouses.

Threshold kept: 15%. Lowered to 5% in the probe, the growth check passed COST, but a 5% bar lets any
steady retailer through and stops being a screen for disruption, which is the whole point of her lens.
