# Buffett memo: Costco Wholesale (COST)

**Decision: Own the business**

**The checks.**
- ROE of 15% or more every year: pass
- Owner earnings positive every year: pass
- Owner earnings grew, first year to last: pass

**Returns on equity.** Charlie, this is the kind of record I like. Return on equity ran 28.3, 25.1,
31.2, 27.8 and 25.8 percent over the five years, never close to our 15% floor. A business that earns
that much on its owners' money year after year, selling groceries and tires at thin markups, has
something competitors can't copy: members who keep paying to come back.

**Owner earnings.** The cash is real. Owner earnings went from 3,501 in the year ending 2022-08-28
to 9,382 in the year ending 2026-08-30, while net income went from 5,844 to 9,226. In the last year
owner earnings ran ahead of net income, even after capital spending of 6,435 on new warehouses.
That spending is mostly growth, not upkeep, so the true owner earnings are better than this row
shows. The one soft spot was the first year, when operating cash flow was only 7,392.

**What I'd need to see.** Returns on equity holding above our floor as equity keeps climbing, from
20,642 to 35,803 over these years. And owner earnings staying ahead of net income: if capital
spending starts eating the cash and the warehouses stop paying for themselves, I'd want to know.
