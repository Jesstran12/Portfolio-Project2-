# Cathie Wood memo: Costco Wholesale (COST)

**Decision: Pass**

**The checks.**
- Revenue grew 15% or more a year, first year to last: fail
- R&D 5% or more of revenue, latest year: not known

**Growth.** Costco is a beautifully run machine, but it is not an exponential one. Revenue went
from 226,954 in the year ending 2022-08-28 to 303,154 in the year ending 2026-08-30, a growth rate
of 7.51 a year. That is steady compounding of a mature model, well short of the 15% a year we look
for in a platform riding a disruptive cost curve.

**Innovation.** R&D is not found in any of the five 10-Ks, so I cannot judge how much Costco
reinvests in innovation from these filings. A retailer may simply not report the line; that is a
gap, not a zero, and I won't fill it.

**What I'd need to see.** Revenue growth well above 7.51 a year driven by something new, such as
e-commerce or membership services scaling on a technology platform, and R&D reported as its own
line so we can see the reinvestment.
