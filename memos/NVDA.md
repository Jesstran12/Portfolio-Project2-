# Buffett memo: NVIDIA (NVDA)

**Decision: Own the business**

**The checks.**
- ROE of 15% or more every year: pass
- Owner earnings positive every year: pass
- Owner earnings grew, first year to last: pass

**Returns on equity.** Charlie, the numbers are almost silly. Return on equity was 36.6, 19.8, 69.2
and then 76.3 percent in the latest year. The dip to 19.8 still cleared our 15% floor, but it tells
you this business can lurch; chips have always been a boom-and-bust trade.

**Owner earnings.** Owner earnings went from 8,132 to 96,676 over the five years. Capital spending
is light, 6,042 in the latest year. But owner earnings ran below net income of 120,067 at the end;
operating cash flow was 102,718, so some of the profit is still sitting in receivables and
inventory rather than the bank.

**What I'd need to see.** Returns holding up through the next downturn in chip demand, not just the
boom, and operating cash flow catching up with net income.
