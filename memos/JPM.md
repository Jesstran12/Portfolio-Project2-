# Buffett memo: JPMorgan Chase (JPM)

**Decision: Watch it**

**The checks.**
- ROE of 15% or more every year: fail
- Owner earnings positive every year: not known
- Owner earnings grew, first year to last: not known

**Returns on equity.** Return on equity ran 16.4, 12.9, 15.1, 17.0 and 15.7 percent. That is a
respectable bank, but it slipped under our 15% floor in the year ending 2022-12-31, so it fails the
check. Net income of 37,676 that year against 48,334 the year before tells me even the best-run
bank feels the cycle.

**Owner earnings.** I cannot judge owner earnings from these filings. Capital spending was not
found in any of the five 10-Ks, so owner earnings are not found and both owner-earnings checks are
not known. For a bank, operating cash flow mostly reflects money moving through the balance sheet,
not cash the owners could take out, so this measure doesn't fit the business anyway. I won't fill
the gap with a guess.

**What I'd need to see.** Return on equity holding at or above our floor through a bad year, not
just good ones. And a measure of a bank's earning power that the filings report directly, since
owner earnings as we compute them can't be read here.
